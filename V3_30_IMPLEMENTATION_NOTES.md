# Kenza Tracker V3.30.0

- Receipt confirmation now saves document verification and case-location updates concurrently, closes immediately after success, and refreshes data in the background.
- Repeated notifications are grouped by case, category and action while the full audit tab remains ungrouped.
- Cases now include per-user pinned cases, a favorites-only filter and a recently viewed shelf.
- Case cards and Quick View include Call, WhatsApp and Share shortcuts.
- Quick View Activity combines case history, payments, appointments, customer deliveries and confirmed custody movements in one chronological scrollable timeline.
- Favorites and recently viewed data are stored per signed-in user in the browser and require no database migration.
